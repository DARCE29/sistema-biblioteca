## 1. Which clip types perform best (reactions, fails, glitches, rage, RP moments, comparisons, news)?

### Takeaway
No reliable cross-type dataset with view counts turned up. The sources do agree on a pattern. Clips that go viral are short and emotion-led: a streamer's reaction or a bizarre or broken moment. GTA RP clips need a little setup line, because "the entertainment is the conversation". For GTA 6, the best-documented attention spike is trailer and launch-adjacent content: the trailers themselves set all-time records.

### Cited Findings
- [VENDOR] RP clips work differently from sandbox clips because "the entertainment is the conversation". RP clips need more lead-in, often the line that set up the scene. The guide's best-known example is xQc breaking character after beating NoPixel owner Koil in a shootout; that one was covered by the press, but no view counts were given. — [AutoClip: How to clip GTA funny moments](https://autoclip.dev/blog/how-to-clip-gta-funny-moments)
- [DATA, press-reported] A 15-second GTA 5 sandbox TikTok from the page "GTA VIRAL" was reported by Dexerto at over 1M views. This is evidence that very short chaos/physics clips can break out. — cited via [AutoClip](https://autoclip.dev/blog/how-to-clip-gta-funny-moments)
- [VENDOR] Reaction-first editing: open on the facecam reaction, then cut to the gameplay that caused it. Recommended for funny moments and bad deaths, where "the emotion carries the clip". — [Eklipse: viral gaming clips](https://blog.eklipse.gg/beginner-guide-2/viral-gaming-clips.html)
- [Opinion, press] Palworld's virality was put down to the game being built for "15–20-second clips of bizarre behaviour"; "on TikTok especially Palworld went global". — [PC Gamer: Palworld is 2024's first breakout hit](https://pcgamer.com/palworld-is-2024s-first-breakout-hit-why-is-it-so-popular) (2024)
- [DATA] GTA 6 Trailer 2 (May 6, 2025): Rockstar said it got 475M views across all platforms in 24 hours, "the biggest video launch of all time". — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/the-latest-grand-theft-auto-6-trailer-has-set-a-major-record-by-racking-up-475-million-views-in-just-24-hours); 69M YouTube views in 24 hours — [RockstarINTEL](https://rockstarintel.com/gta-6-trailer-2-hits-69-million-views-in-24-hours/); 1M YouTube likes in 40 minutes — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-trailer-2-new-fastest-watched-video-record/)
- [DATA] GTA 6 Trailer 1 (Dec 2023): 93M YouTube views in 24 hours — [RockstarINTEL](https://rockstarintel.com/gta-6-trailer-2-hits-69-million-views-in-24-hours/); 117M in 2 days — [eXputer](https://exputer.com/news/games/gta-6-teaser-117-million-views-records/)
- [DATA, conflicting] One report says a GTA 6 launch trailer hit 51M views in 4 days, beating GTA 5's 13-year total for its launch trailer (~34M). — [Tech4Gamers](https://tech4gamers.com/?p=367109). Another site (gtaboom) mentions a "Trailer 3" and 2026 figures that conflict with the other reports. Not verified. — [GTABoom](https://www.gtaboom.com/gta-6-trailers-launched-to-more-views-than-every-june-showcase-trailer-combined-ab32)
- [Press] GTA 5 vs GTA 6 side-by-side comparisons spread widely after Trailer 1. Kotaku cites X accounts NikTek and "GTA 6 Updates", plus a Reddit user who recreated GTA 6 promo shots in GTA 5. No view counts were given. — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/fans-are-marvelling-at-how-much-better-gta-6-looks-compared-to-gta-5-2000729596); [UNILAD Tech](https://www.uniladtech.com/gaming/fans-cannot-get-over-huge-upgrade-in-gta-6-trailer-542218-20231206) (Dec 2023)
- [ANECDOTE, press] On Trailer 2 day, "content creators crashed streaming platforms trying to upload reaction videos". — [GTA6Codex breakdown](https://gta6codex.com/blog/gta-6-trailer-2-full-breakdown)
- [Older, 2021] Classic NoPixel clip roundups centre on streamer personality moments, such as Sykkuno pulling off the golden-laptop hack in a single try and xQc's chaos clips. — [Sportskeeda: Sykkuno clips](https://www.sportskeeda.com/gta/5-sykkuno-s-best-gta-rp-clips-july-2021); [Sportskeeda: xQc clips](https://sportskeeda.com/gta/top-5-xqc-gta-rp-clips-july-2021)

### Inferences
- For GTA 6 week 1, the likely high-performers are: (a) streamer "first time seeing X" reactions, such as the first drive in Vice City or first sight of the map; (b) physics, bug and glitch chaos (the Palworld and GTA VIRAL pattern: under 20 seconds, no context needed); (c) GTA 5 vs GTA 6 comparisons, which have a proven audience from the trailer era; (d) secrets and easter eggs, while discovery is fresh. This follows from the cited pattern; it is not measured.
- RP clips will matter more weeks later, once RP servers exist on GTA 6. On launch they will mostly be single-player or online reactions.

## 2. Layouts: facecam top + gameplay bottom, full gameplay, zoomed crop, two-clip stacked / "satisfying" split-screen

### Takeaway
The standard layout for streamer clips in 2025–2026 is a vertical 9:16 stack: facecam on top and gameplay below (or the reverse), with the facecam kept full-size, not shrunk into a corner. The "story or commentary on top, satisfying or looping gameplay below" split-screen is a separate format, used for retention farming; GTA gameplay (driving, stunts) is a common bottom layer. Every figure for how much retention these layouts gain comes from vendors and lacks a stated method.

### Cited Findings
- [VENDOR] Keep the facecam stacked over the game instead of cropping it away. Facecams shrunk into a corner give the editing tool, and the viewer, less to work with. — [Vizard: keep facecam in shot](https://agent.vizard.ai/how-to/cut-gaming-highlights-that-keep-your-facecam-in-shot.html)
- [VENDOR] Story or commentary on top plus a looping gameplay clip on the bottom holds attention through "constant motion cues". — [FlowShorts](https://flowshorts.app/blog/gameplay-video-generator); [GhostShorts split-screen 2026](https://ghostshorts.com/blog/how-to-make-split-screen-videos-for-tiktok-2026); there is even a GTA 6–specific split-screen guide — [GhostShorts: GTA 6 split screen videos 2026](https://ghostshorts.com/blog/gta-6-split-screen-videos-2026)
- [VENDOR, unverified] Split-screen "72% watch-time lift" (2026), "30–45% longer attention", "1.7x engagement". No independent research is cited. — [GhostShorts](https://ghostshorts.com/blog/how-to-make-split-screen-videos-for-tiktok-2026); [ClipSpeed split-screen guide](https://clipspeed.ai/blog/split-screen-clips-guide.html)
- [DATA/measurement caveat] YouTube changed how Shorts views are counted on March 31, 2025, so retention and view comparisons across that date may not be like-for-like. — [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/youtube-shorts-retention-rate)
- [Practical] Some streamers record a 3840×1080 canvas with game and camera as separate full-quality feeds, so clips can be re-laid out vertically later. — [Ulanzi](https://www.ulanzi.com/en-ca/blogs/news/youtube-gaming-growth-tips)

### Inferences
- For streamer clips: facecam on top (about 35–40% of the frame) and gameplay below, plus burned-in captions, is the safe default. Full-frame gameplay with a blurred fill suits no-facecam moments such as glitches or graphics comparisons. Two stacked clips suit GTA 5 vs 6 comparisons.
- The "satisfying gameplay bottom" format is a different business (story or Reddit narration). It risks YouTube's "inauthentic/repetitive" rules if mass-produced (see section 3).

### Gaps
- No platform-published or independent A/B data comparing layouts.

## 3. Successful clip channels: posting volume, growth, earnings

### Takeaway
"Clipping" became a paid distribution industry in 2025–2026. Big streamers (Kai Cenat, IShowSpeed, N3on) pay armies of clippers per 1,000 views, often through marketplaces such as Content Rewards, Clipping or Whop. Platform ad revenue on Shorts is tiny (roughly $0.01–0.15 per 1,000 views). TikTok pays only for videos over 1 minute. So most clip-channel income comes from campaign or CPM payouts from streamers and brands, not from the platforms. Growth stories favour posting volume over chasing one "perfect" clip.

### Cited Findings
- [DATA-ish, press/census] A CreatorDB census of clip accounts found that Kai Cenat and IShowSpeed together accounted for 97% of all clip views in its sample. — [CreatorDB: The Clip Economy](https://creatordb.app/creator-news/the-rise-of-the-clip-economy/) (via search summary; fetch blocked)
- [Press, unverified] Cenat's top clippers "reportedly clear $100,000 a month". — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creator-news/the-rise-of-the-clip-economy/)
- [Press] N3on paid 303 clippers $1.4M in 5 weeks. — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creator-news/the-rise-of-the-clip-economy/); a weaker source claims about $1M per month — [artae.ai](https://artae.ai/blog/n3on-clipping-economy-million-dollar)
- [Directory] Kai Cenat's clipping program is listed as a paid campaign on Content Rewards: a $10,000 pool split by views across Twitch streams, marathons and events. — [Clyper: clipping programs 2026](https://www.clyper.io/clipping-programs)
- [Press] At Kai Cenat's Streamer U, a brand (Ch@mobile) said two clipping agencies followed the stream and "generated well over 30 million views off of the stream through clipping services". — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/kai-cenats-streamer-u-was-a-masterclass-for-brands-looking-to-tap-content-creators/)
- [VENDOR] Campaign rates: most pay $0.20–$6 per 1,000 views, about $1 typical; direct creator deals pay $20–$200 per clip flat, or about $50 per 100K views (e.g. MrBeast in-house). — [Forkoff: how much clippers earn 2026](https://forkoff.xyz/blog/clipping/how-much-do-clippers-earn-2026); [Lumina Clippers](https://luminaclippers.com/blog/how-much-do-clippers-make); [Whipscribe](https://whipscribe.com/clipping/earn)
- [VENDOR] YouTube Shorts: entering the Partner Program requires 1,000 subscribers plus 10M Shorts views in 90 days, and creators keep 45%. Estimated Shorts RPM is $0.01–$0.07, or $0.03–$0.15 depending on niche, so $1,000 a month takes about 10–33M views. Unedited reuploads of other creators' videos are not counted. — [ClipSpeed: YouTube Shorts clipping money](https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-clipping-money.html); [AutoClip: how much clip channels make](https://autoclip.dev/blog/how-much-do-clip-channels-make)
- [VENDOR] Clips over 60 seconds uploaded as regular videos can earn about $2–8 CPM via mid-rolls (not an official figure). — [ClipSpeed](https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/make-money-clipping-streamers.html)
- [VENDOR, unconfirmed] One blog claims Shorts-only channels applying after Feb 1, 2027 will need double the views to monetize. I could not confirm this against an official YouTube source. — [ClipSpeed](https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-clipping-money.html)
- [DATA, official policy via press] YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" on July 15, 2025, and the definition itself stayed the same. The reused-content policy is unchanged: clips, compilations and reactions remain eligible when they add "significant original input or transformative value". Enforcement is **channel-level**: monetization can be removed from the whole channel. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-monetization-update-spam-not-reaction-channels/550755/); [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/youtube-clarifies-inauthentic-content-policy-changes/); [Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/07/08/youtube-targets-inauthentic-content/)
- [VENDOR] TikTok Creator Rewards pays only for videos **over 1 minute**. Estimated RPM is $0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views; gaming sits in the lower half, around $0.35–$0.70. Copied clips and lightly modified compilations "may be ineligible". — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/); [ttcalculator gaming](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/gaming-tiktok-monetization/)
- [Spanish-language explainer] A "clipero" posts vertical cuts of someone else's content with the owner's permission, for a campaign that pays per view. They do not need TikTok's payment program, and each clip is reviewed by hand before payment. — [ClippersFlow: qué es un clipero](https://clippersflow.com/que-es-un-clipero)
- [Growth, VENDOR via CreatorDB] The clip channel "Nexxo Recaps" reportedly tripled its subscribers in 90 days off 23 clips from other people's streams. — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creator-news/the-rise-of-the-clip-economy/)
- [ANECDOTE] A small-streamer study: the fastest grower posted 29–34 clips, and its best clip reached only about 5.9K views. The author argues consistency beat one-off virality. — [Eklipse: small streamers grow by posting clips](https://blog.eklipse.gg/streaming-tips/content-creation/grow-streaming-channel-clips.html)
- [VENDOR] ClipFlip's own case study of scaling a clipping network to "1 billion verified views". — [ClipFlip](https://www.clipflip.io/blog/clipflip-launch-case-study); further case studies — [ClipFarming.org](https://clipfarming.org/resources/case-studies)

### Inferences
- A new GTA 6 clip channel should plan income in three layers: (1) campaign CPM from GTA streamers or brands (the main source early on); (2) TikTok Creator Rewards on 61–90-second compilations or RP scenes; (3) YouTube Partner Program later, which needs real transformation (captions, commentary, editing) to avoid channel-wide demonetization.
- Platform RPMs mean virality alone pays little. Ten million Shorts views earns roughly $100–1,500.

### Gaps
- No verified Social Blade data for specific clip channels for Cenat, xQc, Speed, Ibai, Auron or Grefg was retrievable; fetches were blocked.
- Posting frequency of top clip channels was not documented. Vendor guides typically say several times a day, but I did not find a citable figure.

## 4. Capitalizing on big launches (GTA 6 trailers, Palworld, Elden Ring)

### Takeaway
Launch moments create enormous, short-lived demand. GTA 6 trailers broke all-time video records, and Palworld content reportedly hit about 400M TikTok views. Speed to post matters, but no source measured "minutes to post" against views.

### Cited Findings
- [DATA] GTA 6 trailer records (see section 1): 475M cross-platform views in 24 hours for Trailer 2, and 93M on YouTube in 24 hours for Trailer 1. — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/the-latest-grand-theft-auto-6-trailer-has-set-a-major-record-by-racking-up-475-million-views-in-just-24-hours); [RockstarINTEL](https://rockstarintel.com/gta-6-trailer-2-hits-69-million-views-in-24-hours/)
- [DATA, unclear period] About 400M TikTok views on Palworld-tagged content (unnamed source, no period stated). Three-quarters of Palworld's YouTube viewers came from Japanese channels. — [StreamHatchet: Palworld success](https://streamhatchet.com/blog/palworld-success-reaches-new-heights) (2024)
- [DATA] Palworld launch: 4M copies in 3 days, 1.29M concurrent players on Steam, 4.76M Twitch hours watched and 433,753 peak viewers on day 1, and +708% search interest on the first weekend. — [StreamHatchet](https://streamhatchet.com/blog/palworld-success-reaches-new-heights); [Tech Wire Asia](https://techwireasia.com/2024/01/video-game-industry-set-for-2024-comeback-as-palworld-searches-jump-over-700-percent/)
- [DATA] GTA V was still the #2 most-watched game on livestreams in 2025 despite the pending sequel. — [RockstarINTEL](https://rockstarintel.com/gta-v-2nd-most-watched-game-2025/)

### Inferences
- In the days before Nov 19, 2026, likely content is countdowns, "everything we know", GTA 5 vs 6, and reactions to the launch trailer. On launch day and week 1, the priority is posting fast: streamer first impressions, bugs, glitches and secrets, within hours.
- Palworld's audience leaned towards Japanese channels on YouTube, which hints that non-English language markets can dominate a launch. That supports a Spanish-language niche.

## 5. Single-streamer vs multi-streamer game niche

### Takeaway
No direct comparative study was found. The evidence shows clip views are extremely concentrated on a few mega-streamers: Cenat and Speed are 97% of CreatorDB's census. Single-streamer channels ride official paid programs, but they compete with hundreds of other clippers. A game-niche channel (GTA 6, many streamers) gets a launch tailwind and is more resilient if one streamer leaves.

### Cited Findings
- [Press] Cenat and Speed are 97% of clip views in the CreatorDB census. Campaigns pay from a shared pool split by views, so many clippers compete. — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creator-news/the-rise-of-the-clip-economy/); [Clyper](https://www.clyper.io/clipping-programs)
- [Press] "Nexxo Recaps" grew by clipping *other people's* streams, a multi-creator recap model. — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creator-news/the-rise-of-the-clip-economy/)

### Inferences
- A hybrid is likely best: the channel brand is the game ("GTA 6 clips in Spanish"), the content draws on several top Spanish-speaking streamers, and the channel joins any paid clipping campaigns those streamers run.
- Single-streamer channels risk losing everything if that streamer's popularity falls or the streamer files copyright strikes. Game-niche channels risk losing views once the launch hype fades.

### Gaps
- No data on retention or subscriber conversion for single-streamer vs game-niche channels.
