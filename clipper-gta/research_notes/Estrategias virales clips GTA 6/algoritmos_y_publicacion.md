## 1. TikTok algorithm 2025–2026: key signals, test-batch distribution, search/SEO, Creator Rewards

### Takeaway
TikTok has never published signal weights. The consistent picture is that a video is shown to a small test audience and expands in waves based on how well it holds attention (completion, rewatches) and on deeper engagement (shares, comments, saves). Follower count is not a direct ranking signal. Search is a major and growing discovery surface, indexed from spoken words, on-screen text and captions. For Creator Rewards, a video must be at least 1 minute long to monetize.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, 2020 disclosure, cited second-hand]** TikTok's 2020 transparency disclosure said follower count and a creator's previous viral videos are **not direct ranking signals**. — [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-the-tiktok-algorithm-works); [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2026-what-works-now)
- **[BLOG]** Completion rate, rewatch rate and early engagement velocity are described as the strongest signals. One blog says the completion "bar" rose from about 50% (2024) to about 70% (2026). This is **unverified and has no TikTok source**. — [Socialync 2026](https://www.socialync.io/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2026-what-works-now); [ClipForge](https://clip-forge.io/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2026-creator-growth-guide)
- **[BLOG]** Test-batch model: each video goes first to a small pool, which blogs put at around 200–500 users, and then expands if it performs well. The pool size is unverified. — [Tokportal](https://www.tokportal.com/learn/tiktok-algorithm-2026); [PostLinkApp](https://postlinkapp.com/blog/tiktok-algorithm-explained)
- **[BLOG, conflicting]** Blogs disagree on who is in the first test pool. One says it is mostly existing followers, starting in late 2025. Another says accounts with zero followers skip the follower stage. A third mentions a vague "new account boost" for the first videos. None cites TikTok. — [Socialync new accounts](https://www.socialync.io/blog/tiktok-algorithm-tips-new-accounts-2026); [Scihub101](https://scihub101.com/technology/how-the-tiktok-algorithm-works-in-2026)
- **[BLOG]** Shares are often ranked as the strongest positive engagement signal, and comments are said to weigh more than likes. TikTok has published no weights. — [Socialync 2026](https://www.socialync.io/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2026-what-works-now); [Socialync comments](https://www.socialync.io/blog/increase-tiktok-comments-engagement-2026)
- **[STUDY, Metricool 2026]** The study covered 2.31M TikTok posts from more than 92k accounts, comparing Jan–Feb 2025 with Jan–Feb 2026. Content volume rose about 80% while **average views fell about 31%**. The For You feed still supplies **72.7% of views**. Traffic from hashtags grew **114% year on year**. — [Metricool press release](https://metricool.com/press-release-tiktok-study-2026/); [ValueYourNetwork summary](https://www.valueyournetwork.com/en/tiktok-reach-2026/)
- **[BLOG, consensus]** TikTok search indexes three layers: **spoken audio** (speech-to-text), **on-screen text** (OCR) and **caption**. The advice is to put the main keyword in all three, early (spoken in the first seconds and visible in the opening frame). Turn on auto-captions. Use Creator Search Insights to find search terms with high demand and few videos. Blogs disagree on how the layers are weighted, and one blog's claim that TikTok "confirmed" audio transcription had no primary citation. — [Darkroom](https://www.darkroomagency.com/observatory/tiktok-seo); [ShortGen](https://shortgen.io/blog/tiktok-seo-2026); [Blitzcut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/tiktok-seo-guide-2026); [Metricool TikTok SEO](https://metricool.com/tiktok-seo/)
- **[OFFICIAL per secondary source]** Since **August 2025, TikTok caps hashtags at 5 per post** and treats them as a weak ranking input. — [Metricool TikTok SEO guide](https://metricool.com/tiktok-seo/) (reported via search summary; verify in the app)
- **[Third-party summaries of program rules]** Creator Rewards Program: the account must be 18+, have **10,000 or more followers** and **100,000 or more views in the last 30 days**. Only **videos of at least 1 minute** earn, and pay is per *qualified* views (RPM). Reported RPMs range from about $0.40 to $1.00 per 1k (one source) to about $0.50 to $2.00 per 1k in the US (another). Qualified views are often only about 50–70% of total views. TikTok publishes no fixed rate. — [TTCalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/guides/creator-rewards-program/); [Postfast](https://postfa.st/blog/tiktok-monetization-requirements); [Creators Agency](https://creatorsagency.co/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-2026)
- **[BLOG]** Gaming clips are named among content types frequently flagged as "unoriginal" or "Not eligible for For You", because the core footage belongs to someone else. Added voiceover, commentary and new text overlays are the suggested fixes. Watermarks from other apps, including CapCut's, can trigger the flag. A held-back post shows the reason in analytics and can be appealed. — [InfluenceFlow](https://influenceflow.io/resources/why-is-my-tiktok-unoriginal-content-flag-appearing-and-how-to-fix-it/); [PDFfarming help](https://intercom.help/pdffarming/en/articles/10318309-my-tiktok-videos-get-good-engagement-but-they-are-not-eligible-for-the-for-you-page-fyp-how-can-i-fix-this)
- **[OFFICIAL guideline, quoted second-hand]** TikTok excludes from For You content that "includes unoriginal or reused material without anything new", for example content carrying someone else's watermark or logo. — [Post-bridge](https://www.post-bridge.com/blog/does-cross-posting-hurt-engagement); [Conbersa](https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/how-to-cross-post-without-getting-flagged)

### Inferences
- For a GTA 6 clip account, the biggest risks are not the algorithm. They are being flagged as **unoriginal** and **music or footage copyright** problems. Every clip needs visible added value: voiceover or reaction, an editorial caption ("hidden detail…", "comparison…"), and a montage that goes beyond a raw capture.
- The rising view counts on long posts and the Creator Rewards 1-minute rule pull in different directions. A practical split is short hooks of about 15–35 seconds to win distribution, plus a few clips over 1 minute (compilations, "top 5", explainers) for future monetization once the account reaches 10k followers.
- Metricool's finding of fewer views per post while hashtag traffic grew 114% suggests **search and topic intent** matter more in 2026. During the GTA 6 launch window, search terms ("GTA 6 secreto", "GTA 6 easter egg", "GTA 6 mapa", "GTA 6 dinero rápido") are likely to be a major source of traffic.

### Gaps
- No primary TikTok document from 2025–2026 with signal weights could be fetched, because newsroom.tiktok.com and support.tiktok.com were blocked in this session. TikTok's "How TikTok recommends videos #ForYou" page (2020) is the only well-known official description.
- Test-pool sizes (200–500), completion thresholds (70%) and the "new account boost" are all unverified vendor claims.
- The official wording of the Creator Rewards rules in 2026 could not be confirmed directly. The rules may vary by country, and LATAM and Spain eligibility should be checked in TikTok Studio.

## 2. YouTube Shorts algorithm 2025–2026: engaged views, viewed vs swiped away, 3-minute Shorts, link to long-form, official statements

### Takeaway
The main official signal YouTube exposes is **"Viewed vs. swiped away"** (in Studio, "How many chose to view"). Product lead Todd Sherman (Creator Insider, Aug 2023) explained that Shorts recommendations rely on viewers actively *choosing* to watch, and that a new Short may spike and then dip while YouTube tests audiences. Since March 31, 2025, the public "views" count includes every start or replay, while **engaged views** (the old, stricter count) drive YPP eligibility and revenue. Since October 15, 2024, Shorts can run up to **3 minutes** if square or vertical. A Short can link to one of the channel's long-form videos via "Related video".

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, 2023, second-hand]** Todd Sherman (Shorts product lead) on Creator Insider: a quick swipe past a Short does not count as a view. Shorts recommendations rely on viewers actively choosing to watch. Focus on the audience, not on gaming the system. A Short may get a burst of early views and then fall off while the system tests for its audience. **Do not delete and re-upload** underperforming Shorts. — [TechCrunch via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/youtube-demystifies-shorts-algorithm-views-161321949.html); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/25/youtube-demystifies-the-shorts-algorithm-views-and-answers-other-creator-questions/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-explains-how-shorts-algorithm-works/494953/); [How2Shout](https://www.how2shout.com/technology/youtube-recently-in-a-video-answering-queries-related-to-shorts.html) *(2023: older, but still the most direct official explanation found)*
- **[OFFICIAL Studio wording, quoted second-hand]** The analytics card "How many chose to view" "highlights the percentage of times that viewers viewed your Shorts versus swiped away". "Shown in feed" counts the times the Short was shown in the Shorts feed. — [Creator Essentials](https://www.creatoressentials.com/glossary/viewed-vs-swiped-away/); [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics/); [Grow Creator](https://growcreator.pro/blog/viewed-vs-swiped-away-youtube-shorts)
- **[BLOG]** No official benchmarks exist. Community rules of thumb treat **about 70% or more "viewed"** as workable and **80% or more** as strong. — [Gyre](https://gyre.pro/blog/understanding-the-youtube-shorts-algorithm-a--guide); [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics/)
- **[OFFICIAL change, reported second-hand]** Since **March 31, 2025**, Shorts "views" count every start or replay with no minimum watch time. The previous metric was renamed **"engaged views"** (shown in Studio Advanced Mode) and remains the basis for **YPP eligibility and Shorts revenue sharing**. — [Gyre view-count update](https://gyre.pro/blog/youtube-shorts-view-count-update-impact-strategy-what-to-do-next); [VidSeeds](https://vidseeds.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-strategy-2026/). *One blog claims that since August 24, 2026 all formats count views at play start. This is uncorroborated; flag it.*
- **[BLOG, conflicting]** One source says watch time has "replaced" swipe rate as the key signal. Most say swipe-away and retention or completion remain dominant. — [Socialync Shorts 2026](https://www.socialync.io/blog/youtube-shorts-algorithm-2026); [Everpop](https://everpop.app/blog/youtube-shorts-algorithm-2026-what-actually-matters)
- **[OFFICIAL, Oct 3, 2024 blog "Tall updates coming to Shorts", reported second-hand]** From **October 15, 2024**, Shorts can be up to **3 minutes** if square or vertical. A 16:9 video is treated as a regular upload. The same post added a "Show fewer Shorts" option and remix templates. — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2024/10/04/youtube-shorts-expands-video-length-to-three-minutes/); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/10/03/youtube-shorts-three-minutes-max-length-redesign-trends-templates/); [Kapwing](https://kapwing.com/resources/youtube-shorts-is-making-a-huge-change-to-its-maximum-video-length-and-it-will-affect-creators)
- **[BLOG]** Length opinions: 45–90 seconds is often said to perform best, versus "obsess over watch time, not length". — [Submagic](https://www.submagic.co/blog/youtube-shorts-length); [Descript](https://www.descript.com/blog/article/how-long-can-youtube-shorts-be)
- **[OFFICIAL, 2023–2024]** Clickable external links in Shorts descriptions and comments were removed in August 2023 to fight spam. Creators can add **one "Related video" link** per Short (YouTube Studio, desktop) pointing to a video on the same channel. Since 2024 the link can point to long-form videos. — [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2023/09/01/youtube-related-links-multi-format-creators-shorts-live-long-form/); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/youtube-enables-linking-shorts-long-form-videos-430655); [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-related-links-connect-shorts-long-videos)
- **[ANECDOTE]** In one creator's test, a linked long-form video gained about 1,400 views from about 12,500 Short views, roughly 10% click-through. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-related-links-connect-shorts-long-videos)
- **[OFFICIAL, July 15, 2025]** YPP policy "repetitious content" was renamed **"inauthentic content"** (mass-produced or repetitive content), described as a minor clarification. The **reused content** policy, which covers clips and compilations, is "unchanged". Reaction and commentary can still be monetized when it adds significant original value. Enforcement applies to the **whole channel**. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-targeting-spam-not-reaction-channels/550755); [Netinfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-clarifies-july-15-policy-update-repetitious-content-rebranded-as-inauthentic-content/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-clarifies-inauthentic-content-policy-changes/); [YouTube Help (YPP policies)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)

### Inferences
- A GTA 6 clip channel that only re-uploads raw gameplay or other people's clips risks a **channel-wide** monetization rejection under the reused content policy. Commentary, edits and analysis are the requirement for YPP, not an option.
- Design every Short to win the first second (the "chose to view" decision). Do not delete underperformers; let them be tested over a longer period.
- Use Shorts as a funnel: pin a Related video link to a longer GTA 6 guide or compilation from each Short.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 Creator Insider or Todd Sherman statement could be retrieved directly. The 2023 Q&A is the latest direct algorithm explanation found.
- Exact official wording of the March 2025 view-count change (YouTube Help page 15424877) could not be fetched.
- I found no official data on how Shorts views convert into long-form views or subscribers in 2025–2026.

## 3. Posting frequency, cross-posting, watermarks and reposting

### Takeaway
The best data (Buffer, 11.4M TikTok posts, 2025) shows more posts per week mainly raises the **chance of a breakout**, not median views per post: 2–5 posts per week is the efficient baseline, and 11 or more per week gives the biggest outliers. I found no rigorous data for "X per day" on new accounts or for Shorts. Cross-posting your own clip is not penalized, but **another platform's watermark** and **unoriginal, reused footage** are.

### Cited Findings
- **[STUDY, Buffer 2025]** The study covered 11.4M TikTok posts from more than 150k accounts. Going from 1 post a week to 2–5 per week raised views per post by up to about 17%. 6–10 per week gave about 29%, and 11 or more gave about 34%. Median views stayed roughly flat at about 500. For accounts posting 11 or more times a week, top posts performed about 31 times better than their median, versus about 7.6 times for once-weekly posters. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/study-shows-2-5-weekly-tiktoks-deliver-biggest-view-increase/558641/); [Buffer frequency guide](https://buffer.com/resources/social-media-frequency-guide/); [WaveVision](https://www.wavevision.io/blog/tiktok-posting-frequency-vs-growth-case-study-what-11-4-million-posts-reveal)
- **[STUDY guidance]** Buffer's January 2026 guide still recommends 2–5 TikToks per week as the baseline. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/social-media-frequency-guide/)
- **[BLOG]** Without published data, blogs suggest 1–3 TikToks per day and 3–7 Shorts per week (VidPal), or 1 per day for solo creators and 2–3 per day for teams (FlowShorts). — [VidPal](https://www.vidpal.ai/blog/how-often-to-post-on-tiktok-instagram-youtube-shorts-2026); [FlowShorts](https://flowshorts.app/blog/how-often-to-post-on-tiktok)
- **[BLOG consensus + platform guideline]** Cross-posting your own content is not penalized, and each platform evaluates its copy independently. Watermarks from other apps (for example, TikTok's on YouTube or Instagram) are the main trigger for reduced reach. Export a clean file per platform and do not use TikTok's "share to" option. Audio licensed on TikTok may not be licensed on YouTube, which can lead to Content ID claims or muted audio. — [Post-bridge](https://www.post-bridge.com/blog/does-cross-posting-hurt-engagement); [Socialync duplication](https://www.socialync.io/blog/avoid-content-duplication-penalties-cross-posting-2026); [CreatorTribune](https://creatortribune.com/cross-post-without-watermark/); [Conbersa](https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/cross-posting-strategy-for-creators)
- **[OFFICIAL, Instagram, reported second-hand]** Instagram removes from recommendations accounts that "primarily post unoriginal content", judged over a rolling 30-day window. — [ReelUploader](https://reeluploader.com/blog/cross-posting-penalty-unoriginal-content)
- **[BLOG, unverified]** Claims that TikTok "Content ID" flags the same video reposted across several accounts as unoriginal. — [InfluenceFlow](https://influenceflow.io/resources/why-is-my-tiktok-unoriginal-content-flag-appearing-and-how-to-fix-it/)
- **[OFFICIAL, YouTube 2023]** Do not delete and re-upload underperforming Shorts. — [How2Shout](https://www.how2shout.com/technology/youtube-recently-in-a-video-answering-queries-related-to-shorts.html)

### Inferences
- For launch week, volume is a lottery ticket. The Buffer data supports posting **many** clips (1–3 per day per platform, or 11 or more per week) to increase the odds of an outlier, as long as quality and originality hold. Expect most posts to sit near the median.
- Use a separate, clean (no watermark) export per platform, with platform-native text and captions. Avoid posting the same file on several accounts you own.
- Reposting: on YouTube, the official advice is to leave underperformers alone. On TikTok there is no official rule. Re-editing a clip with a new hook is safer than re-uploading the identical file.

### Gaps
- No study on daily cadence for brand-new accounts or for YouTube Shorts specifically.
- No primary TikTok text found on whether posting very many times a day reduces per-video distribution.

## 4. Best posting times for gaming audiences (LATAM and Spain) and timing around the GTA 6 launch (Nov 19, 2026)

### Takeaway
Large studies conflict. Buffer (7.1M posts, 2026) favours evenings, weekend mornings and Sunday 9am. Sprout (2026) favours Tuesday–Friday 2–6pm local time. Gaming-specific guides point to **evenings, 19:00–00:00 local**. For a mixed Spain and LATAM audience, time zones dominate, so the account's own analytics should decide after 2–3 weeks. GTA 6 launches **Thursday Nov 19, 2026** (PS5 and Xbox Series X|S), with preloads reportedly from Nov 12.

### Cited Findings
- **[STUDY, Buffer 2026, about 7.1M posts]** No single "magic" time. Evenings and weekends do best, and Sunday 9am is reported as the top slot. Secondary write-ups disagree on details (Saturday first by median engagement; Sunday 9am plus Monday 1pm; 12–5pm weakest). The publication date is also inconsistent (March, July or September 2026). — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok); [IQFluence comparison](https://iqfluence.io/public/blog/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok); [Mubert](https://mubert.com/blog/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok)
- **[STUDY, Sprout Social 2026]** Best times are Tuesday–Friday, 2–6pm audience local time, with weekends weaker. — [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-social-media/); [IQFluence](https://iqfluence.io/public/blog/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok)
- **[BLOG, gaming niche]** Gaming performs best at night, **19:00–00:00** (Carlos Maiz, Spanish guide). Tech and gaming do best on **weeknights 7–9pm** (Sellerpic). — [Carlos Maiz](https://www.carlosmaiz.com/mejor-hora-para-publicar-en-tiktok-guia-completa/); [Sellerpic](https://www.sellerpic.ai/blog/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok)
- **[BLOG, Spanish-language]** Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are cited as the days of highest engagement (Branch, a Colombian agency). — [Branch Agencia](https://branch.com.co/marketing-digital/la-mejor-hora-para-publicar-en-tiktok-2026/); [Cyberclick](https://www.cyberclick.es/numerical-blog/mejores-horas-para-publicar-en-tiktok)
- **[FACT]** GTA 6 is scheduled for **Thursday, November 19, 2026**, per Rockstar after two delays (from 2025, then May 26, 2026). PS5 and Xbox Series X|S are confirmed. PC status conflicts across sources. Digital preloads are reported from Nov 12. — [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/108727/rockstar-officially-delays-gta-6-again-will-now-release-on-november-19-2026/index.html); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-delayed-6-more-months-as-rockstar-commits-to-november-2026-and-says-sorry); [Fox10](https://www.fox10tv.com/2026/10/16/gta-6-locks-nov-19-release-date-after-delays/?outputType=amp)

### Inferences
- **Time-zone math (CET, Spain, in November is UTC+1).** México City is UTC-6, so Spain minus 7h. Colombia, Perú and Ecuador are UTC-5 (minus 6h). Argentina and Chile (summer time) are UTC-3 (minus 4h). One slot cannot hit the evening peak everywhere. Spain at 21:00 is 14:00 in México, 15:00 in Bogotá and 17:00 in Buenos Aires. **México at 20:00 is 03:00 in Spain.**
- Suggested test grid for a Spanish-language account: (a) 19:00–22:00 CET for Spain, (b) about 19:00–22:00 México time (02:00–05:00 CET, scheduled) for México and Central America, (c) a midday or afternoon CET slot, which lands in the LATAM morning and covers the Sprout window. After about 3 weeks, keep what the TikTok and YouTube follower-activity charts show.
- **Launch timing (Nov 19).** Search and feed demand for "GTA 6" will peak in the first 24–72 hours and the first weekend (Nov 20–22). Speed beats polish, because early, searchable clips (first mission, map, secrets, glitches, money) catch the search wave. Pre-launch (Nov 12–18), post preload, hype, prediction and "what we know" clips to seed the topic graph for the account. Note that the launch falls in US Thanksgiving week, with Black Friday on Nov 27, which can affect ad-revenue RPMs. This is an inference with no source on RPM timing.
- Embargoes and spoilers are possible risks. Rockstar and Take-Two have historically taken down leaked footage. I found no source on their 2026 clip policy, so it is a gap.

### Gaps
- No platform-published or large-study data specific to **gaming in Spain or LATAM**. All time studies are global or US-centric.
- No data on how algorithm behaviour changes during mega-launches (for example, competition saturation for "GTA 6" tags).
- Rockstar's policy on monetized GTA 6 clips and footage on launch week was not researched (out of scope).

## 5. Captions, descriptions, hashtags, keywords, Shorts titles and trending sounds

### Takeaway
Keyword placement in **spoken audio, on-screen text and caption** is the most consistent 2026 advice for TikTok search. Hashtags are a weak but positive input. TikTok caps them at 5, and Metricool data shows a small gain from using at least one. On YouTube, write a clear searchable title (100-character limit) and put 3–5 relevant hashtags in the description. #shorts is not required. Trending sounds matter less for gaming clips than in-game audio plus voiceover. On YouTube, music licensed on TikTok can trigger claims.

### Cited Findings
- **[STUDY, Metricool 2026]** Posts with at least one hashtag got about 5% more views and about 9–10% more interactions. — [Metricool press](https://metricool.com/press-release-tiktok-study-2026/); [Metricool TikTok trends](https://metricool.com/tiktok-trends/)
- **[STUDY vendor guidance, inconsistent]** Metricool recommends 3–5 hashtags on one page and says 1–2 well-chosen ones do most of the work on another. TikTok has capped hashtags at 5 since August 2025. — [Metricool how many hashtags](https://metricool.com/how-many-hashtags-to-use/); [Metricool TikTok SEO](https://metricool.com/tiktok-seo/)
- **[BLOG]** A few specific, descriptive hashtags beat broad ones. — [Darkroom](https://www.darkroomagency.com/observatory/tiktok-seo)
- **[BLOG consensus]** Say the keyword in the first seconds, show it as on-screen text in the opening frame, repeat it in the caption, and turn on auto-captions. — [ShortGen](https://shortgen.io/blog/tiktok-seo-2026); [Blitzcut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/tiktok-seo-guide-2026)
- **[YouTube Help, cited second-hand]** If a video has **more than 60 hashtags**, YouTube ignores all of them. Some blogs say 15, which looks wrong. Up to 3 description hashtags can appear above the title. No source says #shorts is required. Shorts titles are capped at **100 characters**. — [HashtagTools](https://hashtagtools.io/blog/youtube-hashtags-shorts-seo-guide-2026); [Taskant](https://taskant.com/youtube-hashtags-guide/); [ContHunt](https://conthunt.app/blog/youtube-shorts-hashtags-title-vs-description); [PostLinkApp](https://postlinkapp.com/blog/youtube-shorts-hashtags)
- **[BLOG]** Audio licensed in TikTok's library may not be licensed on other platforms, which can lead to copyright claims or muted audio. — [Conbersa](https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/cross-posting-strategy-for-creators)

### Inferences
- Caption template, TikTok: `[Keyword phrase in Spanish] + hook/question` plus 3–5 hashtags, for example `#gta6 #gtavi #gta6español #gaming #[topic]`. Mix one broad tag, one or two game tags and one or two specific tags. Shorts: title `GTA 6: [specific searchable thing] 😳` (≤ 60 characters is visible), with 3 hashtags in the description, plus the Related video link.
- On-screen text in the first frame doubles as the hook and as an OCR keyword ("ESTO NADIE LO VIO EN GTA 6").
- Spanish search terms can be less crowded than English ones. Use TikTok Creator Search Insights to find Spanish "content gap" queries during launch week.
- Trending sounds: for gameplay clips, keep game audio and voiceover as the main track, and use a trending sound at low volume only on TikTok. Export the YouTube version without TikTok-library music.

### Gaps
- No official TikTok document on caption length or keyword weighting.
- No study isolating the effect of trending sounds on reach in 2025–2026.

## 6. Engagement tactics: pinned comments, questions, controversy, video replies, series and parts

### Takeaway
The consistent (but mostly vendor-sourced) advice is to reply fast, within about the first hour, turn good comments into **video replies** (each a new post with its own distribution), ask specific questions, and build series. No platform has published effect sizes. Engagement-bait and "controversy" claims have no supporting data.

### Cited Findings
- **[BLOG]** TikTok video replies to comments appear as new videos on the profile, each with independent distribution potential. — [Socialync comments](https://www.socialync.io/blog/increase-tiktok-comments-engagement-2026); [TTCalculator engagement](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/how-to-increase-engagement/)
- **[BLOG, Hootsuite citing an agency CEO]** Use questions from your audience and answer them as video replies. — [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-comments/)
- **[BLOG]** Reply within the first hour. One claim says that waiting 24 hours means the push has likely already ended. Another vendor's "25–40% higher engagement" figure has no source and should not be relied on. — [Socialync comments](https://www.socialync.io/blog/increase-tiktok-comments-engagement-2026)
- **[STUDY, Socialinsider]** Average comments per video rose across all account sizes from 2024 to 2025. Average engagement rate by views was 3.85% in H1 2026, down from 4.20%, though Socialinsider pages conflict on these figures. — [Socialinsider benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-fyp-metrics/); [Socialinsider stats](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-stats/)
- **[BLOG]** Pinning standout replies is recommended, but no measured effect was found. — [Megadigital](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/tiktok-tips-and-tricks/)

### Inferences
- Gaming-specific tactics include "Parte 1/2/3" series on hidden details or the map, "comment which mission next", and video replies to "how did you do that?" comments. These are natural, low-risk comment drivers.
- Pin a comment with a question or the next-part teaser. On YouTube, the pinned comment cannot carry an external link, but the Related video link does the funnel job.
- Polarizing takes ("GTA 6 is overrated?") may drive comments, but no evidence of net benefit was found. They also carry brand and safety risk, so use them sparingly and frame them as debate, not attacks.

### Gaps
- No official data from TikTok or YouTube on the ranking value of comments versus shares versus saves, or on whether video replies or series are favored.

## 7. Account setup: niche, profile and "warming up" a new account (myths vs evidence)

### Takeaway
I found **no reliable evidence** that TikTok requires a warm-up period or shadowbans new accounts. Warm-up schedules (2–7 days of scrolling or liking) come from vendors selling tools. What does matter, per the official principle, is that follower count is not a direct ranking signal and that each video is judged on its own performance. A tight, consistent niche helps the system find the right test audience (inference). Avoiding unoriginal or watermarked uploads from day one matters more than any warm-up.

### Cited Findings
- **[BLOG review]** A 2026 review found no source that establishes a universal waiting period or daily engagement quota that guarantees reach for new accounts. — [Coronium](https://www.coronium.io/blog/tiktok-account-warm-up)
- **[BLOG, vendors]** Recommendations vary: 2–3 days of warm-up for TikTok (Ssemble), no posting for the first 3–7 days (AdaptlyPost), and no promotional video right after account creation (Stormy). All are sales content for those tools. — [Ssemble](https://www.ssemble.com/blog/account-warmup-guide-2026); [AdaptlyPost](https://adaptlypost.com/support/tiktok-warmup); [Stormy](https://stormy.ai/blog/how-to-scale-tiktok-accounts-shadowban-fix)
- **[BLOG]** Hootsuite calls TikTok shadowbans "maybe" real and controversial. Go-Viral calls the common myths unfounded. — [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-shadowban); [Go-Viral](https://www.go-viral.app/blog/shadowban-myths-debunked/)
- **[OFFICIAL 2020, second-hand]** Follower count and past virality are not direct ranking signals. — [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-the-tiktok-algorithm-works)
- **[BLOG]** A post held back from For You shows the reason in analytics and can be appealed. — [PDFfarming help](https://intercom.help/pdffarming/en/articles/10318309-my-tiktok-videos-get-good-engagement-but-they-are-not-eligible-for-the-for-you-page-fyp-how-can-i-fix-this)

### Inferences
- Setup checklist:
  - Use a **personal (creator) account** on TikTok, since Creator Rewards needs a personal account per the program summaries.
  - Choose a handle and name with the keyword ("GTA 6" or "GTA VI" plus a Spanish identity).
  - Write a bio stating the niche and language.
  - Use a consistent visual style and on-screen text style.
  - On YouTube, use a channel name or handle containing "GTA" and links to long-form playlists.
- A light, natural "warm-up" (watching niche content for a day so the device has normal behaviour) is harmless but **unproven**. Do not delay launch-week posting for it.
- Bigger real risks are mass-uploading identical files across several accounts, other-app watermarks, raw reused footage and copyrighted music.

### Gaps
- No primary TikTok or YouTube statement on new-account trust scoring, warm-up periods or limits on posting volume for new accounts.
- No independent controlled experiment on warm-up was found.
