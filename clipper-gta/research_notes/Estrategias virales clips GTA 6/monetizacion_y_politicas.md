# Monetization rules, reused/unoriginal content policies and copyright risk for streamer-clip accounts (TikTok / YouTube Shorts) + Rockstar/Take-Two stance on GTA content

Research date: 2026-11-11. Method note: the session's network blocked direct fetches of support.google.com and support.rockstargames.com, so official-policy text below comes from search-result snippets of those pages plus secondary reporting. Most rate/RPM numbers come from third-party creator-tool blogs (often selling clipping tools): treat them as estimates, not official figures. "Official" vs "anecdote/third-party" is flagged on each item.

## 1. YouTube Partner Program: reused content, the July 2025 "inauthentic content" update, Shorts revenue rules, Shorts RPM (and the Feb 2027 change)

### Takeaway
YouTube did not ban clip channels in July 2025: it renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" (mass-produced/templated videos), and the separate "reused content" policy (which covers clips, compilations, reactions, commentary) was unchanged. Streamer clips are monetizable only when the channel adds meaningful original value (commentary, narrative editing, critique); raw reuploads are not, even with the streamer's permission. Shorts gaming RPM is very low (roughly $0.02–$0.17 per 1,000 views), and from 1 February 2027 Shorts revenue sharing requires 10M qualified Shorts views per rolling 90 days, which is the single biggest structural change for small clip channels.

### Cited Findings
**Reused vs inauthentic content (official + reporting)**
- On 15 July 2025 YouTube renamed its "repetitious content" monetization policy to "inauthentic content," clarifying it covers repetitive or mass-produced videos, which "has always been ineligible" — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-clarifies-inauthentic-content-policy-changes/); [Netinfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-clarifies-july-15-policy-update-repetitious-content-rebranded-as-inauthentic-content/)
- YouTube Help Center (official, via snippet): "there is no change to our reused content policy which reviews content like commentary, clips, compilations, and reaction videos." — [YouTube Help: YPP monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- YouTube said the July update would not specifically target reaction or clip channels, but enforcement would increase against repetitive/spam-like content — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-monetization-update-spam-not-reaction-channels/550755/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/entertainment/youtube/never-fear-reaction-videos-are-still-allowed-under-youtubes-new-inauthentic-content-policy-222401009.html)
- Official definition of violation: taking someone else's content, making minimal changes, and presenting it as your own original work — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
- Official: the policy is applied to the channel as a whole; monetization may be removed from the entire channel if videos violate it or if YouTube "cannot clearly tell you made the content." Allowed example includes using clips for a critical review — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en) (snippet via search)
- The reused content policy applies even if you have permission from the original creator; copyright is a separate layer — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/reused-content-monetization-rejected); [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-reused-content-policy-guide/) (third-party summarizing Help Center)
- Commonly cited "not allowed" examples: compilations of clips (movies, sports, gameplay) with minimal narration; videos primarily of non-verbal reactions without voice commentary. "Allowed": adding funny or insightful commentary; editing that tells a story — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/understanding-youtubes-reused-content-policy); [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/p/youtube-reused-content-policy-guide) (third-party)
- Reused content is acceptable if viewers can discern a "meaningful difference" between the original and your video — [Stack Influence](https://stackinfluence.com/blog/youtube-shorts-monetization) (third-party paraphrase of Help Center)

**Shorts revenue-sharing mechanics (official)**
- Ads between Shorts in the feed are pooled monthly into a Creator Pool that both pays creators and covers music licensing — [YouTube Help: Shorts monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)
- Music deduction: a Short with 1 music track sends 1/2 of its associated revenue to the Creator Pool; with 2 tracks, 1/3 (the rest pays music licensing). Original-audio/no-music Shorts keep the full allocation — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)
- Creators get 45% of the revenue allocated to them from the Creator Pool (widely reported; the official snippet located ties "45%" explicitly to YouTube Premium Shorts revenue) — [Stack Influence](https://stackinfluence.com/youtube-shorts-monetization-in-2025/); [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)
- Ineligible views: artificial/bot views and views on content that conflicts with advertiser-friendly guidelines; Shorts over one minute that contain Content ID-claimed content are blocked and not monetizable — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)
- YouTube's ad-suitability rules were updated to allow certain graphic gameplay to earn ads if it appears after the first ~7 seconds (relevant for GTA violence) — [Gaming Amigos](https://www.gamingamigos.com/post/youtube-updates-monetization-graphic-gaming-gta-6) (secondary; not verified against official page)

**February 2027 YPP / Shorts threshold change (announced ~10 Aug 2026)**
- From 1 Feb 2027, channels need ≥10M qualified Shorts views in the preceding 90 days to receive ads and subscription revenue on Shorts — this applies even to existing YPP members; falling below pauses Shorts revenue (no removal from YPP, no reapplication) — [NewscastStudio](https://www.newscaststudio.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-monetization-changes-2027/); [Exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/industry-briefing-news/youtube-sets-10-million-shorts-views-for-monetisation-from-2027-157245.html); [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/youtube/youtube-announces-monetization-changes-that-make-it-harder-for-new-creators-to-make-money-3396910/)
- New applicants from 1 Feb 2027: still 1,000 subscribers, but 8,000 public watch hours in 12 months (up from 4,000) or 20M Shorts views in 90 days (up from 10M) — [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/417147/youtube-doubles-eligibility-requirements-for-creat.html); [Mediaweek](https://mediaweek.com.au/youtube-doubles-the-hurdle-for-creators-chasing-ad-revenue)
- One source says updated monetization terms must be accepted in YouTube Studio by 31 Jan 2027; not corroborated elsewhere — [Switcher Studio](https://www.switcherstudio.com/whats-new/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027)

**Shorts RPM for gaming (third-party, conflicting)**
- Gaming Shorts RPM $0.02–$0.04 — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-monetization/)
- US gaming Shorts $0.03–$0.10 per 1,000 views — [Shortsync](https://www.shortsync.app/resources/how-much-do-youtube-shorts-pay-2026)
- AIR Media-Tech dataset (274 channels): gaming Shorts median RPM ~$0.17, ≈10–14% of gaming long-form RPM — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/what-is-youtube-shorts-rpm-in-your-niche-in-2026)
- Tubular Labs data: gaming clips ~$0.038 RPM — [Voxbooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/short-form-revenue-share-statistics-2026/)
- Long-form gaming RPM ~$5–$10 vs Shorts $0.05–$0.10 — [Vidpros](https://vidpros.com/youtube-shorts-monetization-in-2026)

### Inferences
- For a GTA clip channel, YouTube Shorts ad revenue is close to negligible: at ~$0.05–$0.17 RPM, 10M Shorts views/90 days ≈ $500–$1,700 per quarter. After Feb 2027, channels below 10M views/90 days earn $0 from Shorts ads, so Shorts become a funnel to long-form compilations/commentary (where RPM is 30–100x higher) rather than an income source.
- Because the reused-content review is channel-wide, one stretch of raw reuploads can demonetize the whole channel; consistency of transformation matters more than any single video.
- Streams usually contain background music; a Short with music gets 1/2 or less of its pool share, and >60s Shorts with claimed content are blocked entirely — muting/replacing stream music is both a copyright and a revenue lever.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch the full official Help Center text (support.google.com blocked in this session), so the exact current list of "allowed/not allowed" reused-content examples is from snippets and third-party paraphrases.
- No official YouTube RPM figures exist; all RPM numbers are third-party and vary ~8x.
- Current (pre-2027) thresholds (1,000 subs + 4,000 hours/12 months or 10M Shorts views/90 days; lower 500-sub fan-funding tier) are background knowledge, consistent with the "doubling" reporting above but not separately sourced here.

## 2. TikTok Creator Rewards Program (2025–2026): eligibility, originality, 1-minute rule, RPM, flags and penalties; Subscriptions/LIVE

### Takeaway
Creator Rewards pays only for original videos over 1 minute from accounts with ~10K followers and 100K views in 30 days, in a small set of countries (per TikTok Creator Academy as reported Sept 2026: US, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico, Brazil). Raw streamer clips, reposts, light edits (speed, filters, stickers, static text), watermarked content, and Duets/Stitches are treated as unoriginal; repeated flags can make an account permanently ineligible. Gaming RPM is roughly $0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views (third-party estimates).

### Cited Findings
- Commonly cited thresholds: 18+, 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in last 30 days, videos ≥1 minute, original content — [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/how-to-join-the-tiktok-creator-rewards-program); [ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/guides/tiktok-monetization-requirements/) (third-party)
- Reaching 10K followers does not make every upload payable; each video must qualify — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/?p=188925)
- Country list per TikTok Creator Academy (reported 10 Sept 2026): US, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico, Brazil; Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Poland not included — [Linkdash](https://linkdash.eu/en/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-europe-2026-realistic-earnings-by-country-uk-13); also [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/?p=188925). Contradicted by [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/how-to-join-the-tiktok-creator-rewards-program), which lists Canada and Italy and not Japan/Korea. Must legally reside in an eligible country with a local payout method.
- Qualified views must come from the eligible markets — [Linkdash](https://linkdash.eu/en/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-europe-2026-realistic-earnings-by-country-uk-13)
- TikTok's guidelines (as reported) treat reposts with light changes — speed shifts, filters, fixed text, stickers — as unoriginal — [Baller Alert](https://balleralert.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-disqualification/)
- Duets and Stitches are not counted as original for the program; streamer clip programs that grant rights do not by themselves make clips eligible for Creator Rewards — [FindClout](https://findclout.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-reposted-clips)
- Other platforms' watermarks/logos (and visible streamer overlays) count against originality and also hurt For You distribution — [cut.pro](https://cut.pro/en/blog/tiktok-stuck-at-200-views-2026); [FindClout](https://findclout.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-reposted-clips)
- Low-resolution, static or slideshow-style content is rejected — [ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/guides/tiktok-monetization-requirements/)
- Penalties (third-party, conflicting): 5 ineligible videos within 30 days can disqualify; 5 account-level violations = permanently ineligible — [Clip-Short](https://www.clip-short.com/guides/tiktok-creator-rewards-program); creators report permanent loss after ~3 unoriginal-content violations (described as anecdotal) — [Social Boost Digital](https://socialboostdigital.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-unoriginal-content-flag). Appeal windows cited: 80 days per video, 30 days per account (third-party).
- Some original creators report being falsely flagged with no clear appeal path (anecdote) — [Baller Alert](https://balleralert.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-disqualification/)
- RPM: gaming $0.40–$1.00 — [ttcalculator gaming](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/gaming-tiktok-monetization/); gaming typical $0.55 (range $0.25–$0.90) — [ttcalculator niches](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/rpm-rates-by-niche/); all-niche US median $0.92 in early 2026 vs $0.68 in Q4 2025 — [ttcalculator Q1 2026](https://ttcalculator.net/blog/q1-2026-tiktok-rpm-report/); international audiences 40–70% lower — same source family (third-party)
- Europe realistic RPMs by country cited as UK £1.30, DE €0.80, FR €0.70 — [Linkdash](https://linkdash.eu/en/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-europe-2026-realistic-earnings-by-country-uk-13)

### Inferences
- A pure "cut and post" streamer clip account is very likely to be flagged on TikTok; to qualify a clip must be >60s and carry an original layer (voiceover/commentary, narrative re-cut, multiple sources, own analysis). Short 15–30s viral clips cannot earn Creator Rewards regardless of originality.
- At ~$0.50 RPM, 1M qualified views ≈ $500; much less if the audience is outside eligible markets.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm TikTok's primary Creator Rewards terms text for exact violation thresholds.
- No usable sources gathered on TikTok Subscriptions and LIVE Gifts requirements/2026 splits for clip accounts (background: LIVE requires 18+ and ~1,000 followers; Subscriptions has its own follower threshold — unverified here). A clip account has little natural use for LIVE gifts unless it hosts its own streams/watch-alongs.

## 3. Copyright: streamer DMCA/Content ID, Twitch/Kick terms, music in streams, permission models; Take-Two/Rockstar policy and GTA 6 (leaks, embargo, spoilers)

### Takeaway
Clip accounts face three independent copyright holders: the streamer (their stream footage), music labels (background music in streams), and Take-Two (GTA footage). Rockstar's official policy tolerates fan gameplay footage but defines permission as "non-commercial" and explicitly targets pre-release/leaked footage, endings, story-spoiler and cutscene compilations; Take-Two aggressively DMCA'd and subpoenaed platforms over the August 2026 GTA 6 leaks. GTA 6 launches 19 November 2026 (reported review embargo 18 November); spoiler-heavy content is the highest-risk category in the launch window.

### Cited Findings
**Rockstar / Take-Two (official policy via snippets, last updated 10 Jan 2025)**
- Rockstar is "happy for fans to upload footage of their gameplay," and does not object to non-commercial uses "in a manner which does not intentionally spoil the plot" — [Rockstar Support: Policy on posting copyrighted Rockstar Games material](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/7bNaeoMFTV0iUDGhStTXvz/policy-on-posting-copyrighted-material)
- "'Non-commercial' means that you don't make money through the game footage you post or use the material as part of a promotion for a product or service." Take-Two reserves the right to take material down at any time; questions to copyright@take2games.com — [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/200153756/Policy-on-posting-copyrighted-Rockstar-Games-material)
- Takedown categories: pre-release footage incl. leaked clips and early unboxings ("regardless of how the game was obtained"); endings, edited cutscene/dialogue compilations, major story reveals; isolated cinematics; in-game TV/comedy shown in isolation; footage encouraging online cheating or promoting mods/ports. Tolerated: cutscenes within a larger playthrough; narrated Let's Plays — [tbreak](https://tbreak.com/gta-6-spoilers-rockstar-video-takedown-policy/); [GameSpot (GTA V launch, 2013 — older)](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/rockstar-outlines-user-upload-policy-on-gtav-launch-day/1100-6414525/)
- No Rockstar statement found explicitly authorizing ad monetization of GTA footage (in practice monetized GTA channels are widespread and tolerated, but the policy text does not grant it) — [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/200153756/Policy-on-posting-copyrighted-Rockstar-Games-material); compare [VideoGamer (2013 clarification, older)](https://www.videogamer.com/news/rockstar-clarifies-gta-5-youtube-copyright-policy)
- GTA 6 leaks: clips attributed to "Cyberleek" circulated from ~18 Aug 2026, ~17 minutes total by 25 Aug; Take-Two issued DMCA notices across platforms — [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-gameplay-dmca/); [Cyber Magazine](https://cybermagazine.com/news/gta-vi-footage-leak-and-take-twos-legal-fightback-explained)
- Take-Two filed DMCA subpoena applications (incl. to X and YouTube/Google) to unmask leakers; two granted — [TorrentFreak](https://torrentfreak.com/take-two-expands-gta-6-leak-hunt-with-dmca-subpoenas/); [Digital Citizen](https://www.digitalcitizen.life/take-two-gta-6-leak-subpoenas-court-record/) (lower-quality)
- A Rockstar takedown on YouTube yields a copyright strike, not a Content ID claim — [Prepublish](https://prepublish.ai/blog/gta-6-leaks-youtube-dmca) (secondary)
- Sept 2026: takedowns over early GTA VI map images from a surfaced archive; a GTA V mod recreating the GTA 6 map was DMCA'd on YouTube — [Techtroduce](https://www.techtroduce.com/take-two-dmca-early-gta-vi-map-leak); [Harvard TagTeam/feed](https://tagteam.harvard.edu/hub_feeds/3425/feed_items/13524446/content)
- Report that streaming GTA 6 on launch day could trigger automatic strikes (speculative) — [AllKeyShop](https://www.allkeyshop.com/blog/gta-6-launch-day-streaming-copyright-strikes-news-d/)
- Launch 19 Nov 2026 confirmed by Rockstar (PS5/Xbox Series X|S); review embargo reportedly 18 Nov (NateTheHate; verified by Insider Gaming; not officially confirmed) — [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/grand-theft-auto-6-review-embargo/); [GameWatcher](https://www.gamewatcher.com/gta-vi-review-copies-reportedly-sent-out-as-rockstar-keeps-november-18-embargo)

**Twitch / Kick / music**
- Twitch: you may not include music you don't own in streams or on-demand content, including clips; Twitch may mute VODs and delete Clips when audio recognition detects copyrighted audio; rights holders can request removal — [Twitch Music Guidelines](https://www.twitch.tv/p/en/legal/community-guidelines/music); [Twitch Creator Camp](https://twitch.tv/creatorcamp/paths/getting-started-on-twitch/copyrights-and-your-channel)
- June 2020: mass DMCA wave against Twitch clips over background music, with strikes issued (older, but shows clip-level music risk) — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/twitch-dmca-apology-streamers-copyright-184637823.html); [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2020/06/08/twitch-dmca-takedowns-tos-changes/)
- Twitch ToS grants Twitch a broad license; users retain ownership — i.e., the streamer (not Twitch) owns their stream content and can DMCA reuploads — [ConductAtlas](https://conductatlas.com/platform/twitch/twitch-terms-of-service/perpetual-user-content-license/) (third-party summary)
- Kick made its Clipping Program official (Nov 2025); streamers opt in and clippers apply via Kick's Discord; 3B+ clip views in Sept 2025 — [win.gg](https://win.gg/kick-clipping-program/)
- Some streamers register VODs in Content ID (via MCNs), usually set to monetize, so the streamer collects revenue from clips — [Autoclip](https://autoclip.dev/blog/understanding-content-id-as-a-clipper) (vendor blog)
- Rights holders chose to monetize >90% of Content ID claims in 2024 rather than block — [Dynamoi](https://dynamoi.com/learn/faqs/using-copyrighted-music-and-still-monetizing)
- Licensed-clip marketplace "Collab Split" (Oct 2025): pay only a share of revenue tied to the licensed clip — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/10/31/collab-clips-clip-licensing-reaction-content/)
- Fair use is a defense, not a permission — [Autoclip](https://autoclip.dev/blog/understanding-content-id-as-a-clipper)

### Inferences
- Safest permission model: clip only streamers who run an explicit clip program (Kick Clipping Program, Whop/Content Rewards campaigns, streamer clipper Discords), which effectively licenses use. This solves the streamer's copyright but not YouTube "reused content" or TikTok originality.
- For GTA 6 specifically (launch 19 Nov 2026): avoid leaked/pre-release footage entirely (strike + subpoena exposure), avoid ending/story-reveal clips and isolated cutscene compilations, and treat early-launch-window spoilers as high risk. Funny/chaotic gameplay moments, reactions and commentary fit the "tolerated" pattern.
- Mute or replace stream background music; it is the most common automated trigger (Content ID on YouTube, audio recognition on Twitch/TikTok).

### Gaps
- No Kick ToS text on third-party reuploads found; no official Twitch statement on third-party reupload of clips.
- No GTA 6-specific Rockstar statement on streaming/spoiler windows found.

## 4. Spanish-speaking regions: Creator Rewards and YPP availability in LATAM/Spain

### Takeaway
On TikTok, Mexico is the only Spanish-speaking eligible market for Creator Rewards (plus Brazil in LATAM); Spain, Argentina, Colombia, Chile are not on the Creator Academy list reported Sept 2026, and qualified views must come from eligible markets — so a Spanish-language GTA clip account monetizes on TikTok mainly via Mexican viewers. YouTube YPP is broadly available across Spain and Latin America, making YouTube (plus campaigns) the more accessible path for Spanish-language clippers.

### Cited Findings
- Creator Academy list (Sept 2026): US, UK, DE, JP, KR, FR, MX, BR; Spain not included — [Linkdash](https://linkdash.eu/en/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-europe-2026-realistic-earnings-by-country-uk-13)
- Spain and Italy reportedly lost eligibility in the transition from Creator Fund to Creator Rewards — [ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-fund-countries/); conflicting: some guides still list Spain/Italy — [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/how-to-join-the-tiktok-creator-rewards-program)
- No reliable evidence Argentina, Colombia or Chile are eligible in 2026 — [MonetizedNow](https://monetizednow.com/creator-rewards-countries); [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/tiktok-earnings-europe-2026)

### Inferences
- A Spain- or Argentina/Colombia-based operator likely cannot join Creator Rewards at all (residence + payout requirement), regardless of audience.

### Gaps
- YPP country availability could not be verified against YouTube's official list (support.google.com blocked). Background knowledge: YPP covers Spain and virtually all LATAM countries; verify on YouTube Help "YPP availability."
- Spanish-language/LATAM-specific RPMs not found; generally lower than US.

## 5. Alternative monetization: clipping campaigns (Whop Content Rewards, Kick, streamer-paid), affiliate, selling channels, sponsorships

### Takeaway
For streamer clips, paid clipping campaigns are the realistic primary income: typical Whop Content Rewards rate ~$1 per 1,000 verified views (range $0.05–$6), streamer-direct programs (e.g., N3on ~$0.40–$0.50 per 1,000), and brand-sponsored clip campaigns (~$1/1K with caps). These pay per view regardless of platform monetization status, which is why many clip accounts don't depend on YPP/Creator Rewards at all.

### Cited Findings
- Whop: 50 live campaigns (28 Sept 2026) median $1.00 per 1,000 views, range $0.05–$3.00 — [Built by Foundry](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/what-is-clipping-1-dollar-per-1000-views-50-campaigns)
- Most Whop campaigns $0.50–$1.50/1K (July 2026) — [ClipAffiliates](https://www.clipaffiliates.com/blog/clipping-cpm-rates-2026); range $0.20–$6, average ~$1 — [OpenClip](https://openclip.app/guides/whop-clipping-guide); Oct 2026 snapshot $1.25 and $3/1K with $1K–$10K budgets — [Opus](https://www.opus.pro/blog/whop-content-rewards)
- Music campaigns pay less (e.g., Lil Baby campaign $0.30/1K) — [Reap](https://reap.video/blog/whop-clipping)
- Paid only for approved clips and verified views; ~7% payout fee reported; realized rate lower than advertised after rejections/unverified views/thresholds — [Whop docs](https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/growth-marketing/content-rewards); [Content Rewards](https://contentrewards.com/articles/clipping-payouts-explained); [OpenClip](https://openclip.app/guides/how-much-do-clippers-make)
- Whop docs: UGC typically pays more than clipping — [Whop docs](https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/growth-marketing/content-rewards)
- N3on paid >$1.4M to 303 clippers over five weeks; rate $40 per 100K views (sometimes $50/100K); network run with Adin Ross, part reportedly paid by Kick (Kick has not confirmed) — [Tubefilter (Apr 2026)](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/29/n3on-spending-millions-stream-clippers-tiktok-kick/amp/); [cut.pro](https://cut.pro/en/blog/kick-pays-clippers-directly-2026); [OpenClip Kick](https://openclip.app/guides/clipping-on-kick)
- Sponsor-funded clips: Expedia campaign $1/1K views, $10 minimum, $500 cap per clip — [FindClout](https://findclout.com/blog/kick-clipping-program)

### Inferences
- Rough math: a clip account doing 10M views/month on campaigns at $1/1K ≈ $10K gross vs. ~$500–$1,700 from YouTube Shorts ads for the same views — campaigns dominate by an order of magnitude.
- Campaign clips still must survive platform originality systems to get views (TikTok suppresses watermarked/unoriginal clips), so transformation still matters.

### Gaps
- No reliable sources gathered on affiliate programs (e.g., game-key/peripheral affiliates) for GTA clip accounts, on channel-selling prices/marketplaces, or on sponsorship rates for clip pages; buying/selling accounts also violates TikTok/YouTube terms (not verified here).
- No GTA 6-specific clipping campaign rates found (Rockstar itself does not run them).

## 6. Transformation checklist clip channels use to stay monetized

### Takeaway
No platform publishes a numeric "% transformation" rule; reviewers judge whether the channel adds meaningful original value and whether YouTube/TikTok "can clearly tell you made the content." The consensus checklist from official wording and creator guides: original voice commentary or analysis, narrative editing (re-cut, context, setup/payoff), multi-source compilations with a theme, removing other platforms' watermarks/overlays, original or no music, and length >60s for TikTok Creator Rewards.

### Cited Findings
- Official baseline: minimal changes presented as your own = violation; adding critical review/commentary can monetize; non-verbal reactions and minimal-narration compilations don't — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/understanding-youtubes-reused-content-policy)
- Light edits (speed change, filters, static text, stickers) are explicitly not enough on TikTok — [Baller Alert](https://balleralert.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-disqualification/)
- Remove watermarks/streamer overlays; avoid Duets/Stitches for Creator Rewards — [FindClout](https://findclout.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-reposted-clips); [cut.pro](https://cut.pro/en/blog/tiktok-stuck-at-200-views-2026)
